Privacy Policy – Musmach (מוסמך)

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. General

Musmach (מוסמך) is a digital platform for learning, practice and preparation for licensing exams in the health professions, operated by Mohammad Garra ("Musmach" or "we"), through the app and the website musmach.co.il.

We respect our users’ privacy and attach great importance to protecting their personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect, the purposes for which it is used, how it is kept and the rights of users, in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and the regulations made under it.

The controller of the database is Mohammad Garra.

2. Information we collect

The following information is collected when you use the website and the app:

Account and sign-in details

  • Email address.
  • Account identification and sign-in data.
  • The password is managed by the sign-in service provider and is not stored by us as plain text.

Learning activity data

  • Answers to questions and exercises.
  • Questions saved to favorites.
  • Results of practice exams and simulations.
  • Progress data and learning statistics.
  • Reports of errors in the content.
  • The exam date chosen by the user.

Device data

  • Device identifier and device model.
  • Data needed to manage access to the account and to limit its use to two devices.

Website usage data

  • Pages viewed on the website.
  • How you reached the website.
  • Device type.
  • Browser language and time zone.
  • A random identifier stored in the browser to measure activity on the website.

The visit measurement system is not designed to identify the user by name or by email address. Infrastructure providers may process additional technical data as part of operating and securing the service.

Subscription and purchase data

  • Subscription status.
  • Details of the track purchased.
  • Transaction data needed to manage the subscription.

Payments made through the Apple App Store or Google Play are processed by the relevant payment providers. Musmach does not receive full credit card details for these purchases.

3. Providing information

You are not legally required to provide personal information, and doing so depends on your wishes and consent.

However, without the information needed to create and manage an account, it may not be possible to sign up, purchase a subscription, save learning progress or use some of the services.

4. How we use information

The information collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing the learning services and content.
  • Saving progress and learning history.
  • Showing personal statistics.
  • Managing subscriptions, purchases and access permissions.
  • Improving the quality of the questions and learning content.
  • Improving the user experience and system performance.
  • Preventing account sharing and unauthorized use.
  • Finding technical problems and supporting users.
  • Keeping the system secure.
  • Complying with the law.

We do not sell personal information to third parties and do not use personal information for advertising.

5. Transfer of information outside Israel

As part of using cloud services and international service providers, personal information may be stored or processed on servers located outside the State of Israel.

Such transfer of information will be made in accordance with the law that applies to transferring personal information outside Israel.

6. Disclosure to third parties

Apart from transferring information to service providers to operate the system, personal information may be disclosed to third parties in the following cases:

  • Where there is a legal obligation to disclose the information.
  • Following a court order or a lawful demand by a competent authority.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse or harm to system security.
  • To protect legal rights.
  • Where the user has consented to the disclosure.

Information will be disclosed in accordance with the law and only to the extent needed for the relevant purpose.

7. Information security

We are committed to acting in accordance with the information security requirements set by law and to taking suitable technological and organizational measures to protect personal information.

These measures are designed to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, misuse, loss or exposure of information.

However, absolute protection against all information security risks cannot be guaranteed.

Users must keep their sign-in details confidential and let us know if they suspect unauthorized use of their account.

8. Keeping information and deleting your account

Account data and learning activity are kept as long as the account is active and as needed to provide the service.

You can ask to delete your account through:

Account → Delete account.

After the account is deleted, the personal information linked to it will be deleted in accordance with the law and the system’s deletion processes.

Information that must be kept under a legal or accounting obligation, or for handling disputes and information security, may be kept for the period required.

Deleting the account may cause permanent loss of learning history, exam results and progress data.

Deleting the account does not necessarily cancel an active subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Such a subscription is cancelled through the subscription settings of the relevant store.

9. Your rights

Under the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment No. 13 to the Law, users may:

  • Review the personal information held about them, in accordance with Section 13 of the Law.
  • Ask to correct or delete information that is not correct, complete, clear or up to date, in accordance with Section 14 of the Law.
  • Ask to delete their account and the information linked to it, subject to the law.
  • Contact us with any question about the collection and use of information.

Requests to exercise these rights can be sent to the email address:

Musmachrishui@gmail.com

To protect users’ privacy, identity verification may be required before a request is handled.

10. Use by minors

The platform is intended mainly for adult students and graduates in the health professions.

Use of the system by anyone under 18 is subject to the consent of a parent or guardian, where required by law.

If we learn that personal information was collected from a minor contrary to the law, we will handle the information in accordance with the law.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time following changes in the service, in technology or in the law.

The current version will be published on the website and in the app, together with the date of the last update.

In the case of material changes that affect users’ privacy, suitable notice will be given and further consent will be requested where required by law.

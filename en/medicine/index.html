A 58-year-old man collapses at a sports event. A bystander performs CPR and an AED arrives, analyzing a shockable rhythm (VF). What is the immediate step?

  1. A.Deliver one shock and immediately resume chest compressions for 2 minutes
  2. B.Deliver one shock and immediately resume chest compressions for 10 minutes
  3. C.Deliver one shock and intubate before resuming compressions
  4. D.Check for a pulse for a full minute before shocking
Show answer and explanation
Correct answer: A. In cardiac arrest with a shockable rhythm (ventricular fibrillation), the immediate step is a single defibrillation shock followed at once by resumption of chest compressions for 2 minutes.

On day 4 after an inferior MI, a patient develops severe dyspnea, BP 80/50, a new holosystolic apical murmur and pulmonary edema. Echo shows a hyperdynamic LV and a flail mitral leaflet. What is the mechanism?

  1. A.Left ventricular free wall rupture with tamponade and no murmur
  2. B.Ventricular septal defect with a holosystolic murmur at the sternal border
  3. C.Left ventricular aneurysm
  4. D.Posteromedial papillary muscle rupture
Show answer and explanation
Correct answer: D. Acute mitral regurgitation with pulmonary edema and hypotension 3-7 days after an inferior myocardial infarction indicates papillary muscle rupture.

A 38-year-old man with HIV, CD4 count 60/uL, has 2 weeks of dry cough and exertional dyspnea. PaO2 is 58 mmHg on room air, LDH is high and chest CT shows bilateral ground-glass opacities. Bronchoalveolar lavage confirms Pneumocystis jirovecii. What is the treatment?

  1. A.TMP-SMX without steroids
  2. B.Fluconazole
  3. C.TMP-SMX plus prednisone
  4. D.Azithromycin
Show answer and explanation
Correct answer: C. Pneumocystis pneumonia with hypoxemia (PaO2 below 70 mmHg on room air) is moderate-to-severe disease: treat with TMP-SMX for 21 days plus adjunctive corticosteroids (prednisone) started within 72 hours.
